A clearer signal

Reputation, made legible.

Four steps turn public launch history into context the community can actually use.

01

Track every Clanker launch

We scan the most recent launches (up to 500) from Clanker's public API feed on Base, refreshed continuously.

02

Read the full picture

Pair each creator's launches with live market activity — 24h volume, market cap, and holder counts.

03

Calculate a transparent score

Combine those signals on a logarithmic scale, plus a bonus for Clanker's verified flag, into an estimated Trust Score.

04

Find the real builders

Give the community a faster way to discover creators with a consistent track record.

How the score is calculated: Score = 40 × volume factor + 30 × market-cap factor + 15 × holders factor + up to 10 for launch count + 5 if the creator is verified on Clanker — capped at 100. Each factor is log-scaled: 24h volume saturates near $100K, best market cap near $1M, holders near 1,000. Status thresholds: 55+ Trusted · 28–54 Builder · below 28 Risky. The window is the 500 most recent launches, so scores move with the market.
Disclaimer: The Trust Score is a reputation signal — not a safety guarantee or financial advice.
